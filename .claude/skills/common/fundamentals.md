# Social Media Fundamentals

Shared concepts every skill assumes. Not a market-stats file — no figures live here.

## The social loop

```
audience & goal  →  platform mix  →  pillars  →  posts  →  distribution  →  measurement
        ↑                                                                          │
        └──────────────────────────── learn & repeat ──────────────────────────────┘
```

Every skill sits on one stage. A skill should name the stage it acts on and not silently do
another stage's job.

## The four content pillars

A sustainable account mixes four purposes. A calendar should cover all four roughly evenly:

| Pillar | Purpose | Typical formats |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Educate** | teach something useful | how-to, tip, teardown, carousel |
| **Entertain** | earn attention and sharing | trend, meme, story, behind-the-scenes |
| **Inspire** | create aspiration and identity | results, transformation, vision |
| **Convert** | drive a specific action | offer, launch, testimonial, demo |

Pillars are a planning frame, not a scoring input.

## Reach vs. engagement vs. conversion

- **Reach / impressions** — how many saw it. A distribution metric.
- **Engagement** — likes, comments, shares, saves, clicks. A resonance metric. **Saves and
  shares signal value**; likes are the weakest.
- **Conversion** — the business action (click, sign-up, sale). The only metric tied to money.

Rate = numerator ÷ impressions. The denominator must be named (impressions, reach, followers)
or the rate is meaningless.

## Organic vs. paid

- **Organic** builds the audience and the brand; it compounds slowly.
- **Paid** buys reach now; it is a media-buying discipline with its own targeting, creative and
  bidding vocabulary. Keep the two conversations separate.

## The platform-spec layer

Every platform imposes hard constraints (character limits, hashtag caps, aspect ratios,
durations) and soft conventions (hashtag ranges, link handling). Hard constraints are
compliance; soft conventions are guidance. Both come from `dsocial specs` — never from memory.

## Naming things

- **Hook** — the first line or first second that earns the scroll-stop.
- **Caption** — the text body of a post.
- **CTA** — the single action you ask for.
- **UGC** — content created by users/customers, with rights cleared.
- **Crisis** — a fast-moving, reputational event that needs a response playbook.

## What this pack is not

Not a scheduler, not a scraper, not an ads console, not a trend-decoder. It plans, drafts,
audits and narrates. Execution (posting, spending, DM automation) lives elsewhere.
